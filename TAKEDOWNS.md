# Takedowns

Every takedown notice this site receives is published here, with our response. Send notices to [notafriendof@siena.codes](mailto:notafriendof@siena.codes).

## How notices are handled

- **About anything other than tagline text**, such as styling, naming, or a page that implies endorsement: we comply promptly.
- **About tagline text only:** the named content stays down while we get a lawyer's read, and we file a counter-notice only if they agree it is sound.
- **Every notice is published here** with our response, with private individuals' contact details redacted. Notices sent to us by email also go to the [Lumen database](https://lumendatabase.org/). GitHub publishes the notices it receives in [github/dmca](https://github.com/github/dmca).
