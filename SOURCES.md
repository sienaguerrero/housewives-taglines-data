## Miami's eras

Miami has two eras, decided the way RHONY's were: by whether the cast carried over, not by network. S1 to S3 aired on Bravo. After eight years off, S4 and S5 aired on Peacock and S6 onward on Bravo again. S3 to S4 kept Lisa Hochstein and Alexia Nepola, and Larsa Pippen returned from S1, so S4 starts a `revival`, not a `reboot`. The move back to Bravo at S6 kept all six housewives, so it is no era boundary at all.
